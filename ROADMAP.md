# Roadmap

What's coming to Smartivi and what we're thinking about. It's the same list you vote on in the app, under Settings → Help and feedback. Something missing? Start a thread in [Discussions](../../discussions) or write to [support@smartivi.app](mailto:support@smartivi.app).

## Coming next

Decided, and being built.

- **Recordings** _(version 1.1)_: The Mac at home records what you schedule from any device, and you watch it on the Apple TV, iPhone or iPad.

## Ideas we're weighing

Vote for them in the app (Settings → Help and feedback): the most wanted go first.

- **Downloads**: Movies, series and catch-up to watch offline on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
- **Profiles for the family**: Favorites, history, continue watching and parental controls for each person.
- **MAC portal playlists**: Add a playlist with a portal address and a MAC, as MAG boxes use.
- **Live subtitles in your language**: Subtitles made on the device for any channel, translated into your language.
- **Fuller movie details**: Cast, trailers and skip intro for movies and series.
- **Watch together with SharePlay**: The same channel with friends on FaceTime, in step.
- **Radio in CarPlay**: Your radio stations and favorites on the car's screen.
- **Android and Google TV**: Smartivi on Android phones, Google TV and Fire TV, with the same playlists.
- **LG and Samsung TVs**: The app on the TV itself, without an Apple TV.

## Done from your votes

Ideas that came from this list and are already in the app.

- **Your iPhone as the remote** _(version 0.8)_: Choose a channel on your phone and watch it on the Apple TV or Mac.
- **Follow your teams** _(version 0.8)_: Their matches found in your guide, with a reminder.

Plans can change: nothing here is a promise of a date.
