# Changelog

Every version of Smartivi since the first line of code, on 30 October 2026, newest first. The app shows the same notes in Settings → What's new, and they're also on [smartivi.app/changelog](https://smartivi.app/changelog/).

## Version 1.0

14 November 2026 · _On its way to the App Store_

- Fill in channels without a guide: for channels that no guide covers, Smartivi uses a public guide of their country, and offers it when a country has many. Part of Smartivi Premium.
- Smarter guide matching for US channels, with East and West feeds told apart.
- Welcome screens the first time you open Smartivi.
- Categories by country, a quick guide inside the player and bigger text on the Mac.
- Smartivi Premium: monthly, yearly with a free week, or lifetime. Smartivi Basic stays free.

## Version 0.8

5 November 2026

- Smartivi now speaks Portuguese, French, German, Italian and Turkish.
- Vote for what’s next: Settings → Help and feedback. Ideas we’re weighing, one vote each with your iCloud account, and “Send us an idea”.
- Your iPhone or iPad as the remote of Smartivi on your Apple TV or Mac: choose a channel from the list on your phone and it plays on the TV, with pause, channel up and down and close. The remote button is at the top of TV.
- Categories by kind: a playlist’s dozens of groups folded into Sports, Movies, Series, News, Kids… Settings → Playlists → your playlist.
- Follow your teams: Settings → Your teams. Their matches in your guide this week appear in “Your teams” (guide and TV), and each one becomes a reminder.
- Search with a sentence: “where is Madrid playing tonight?”, “a scary one tomorrow”, “documentaries now”. With Apple Intelligence it also understands freer sentences.
- Settings → TV, Movies and Series: the category the guide opens in, favorites first in each category, where Movies and Series open and their order, and clear each section’s history.
- iPad and Mac: the side menu of Apple TV. Icons when closed, names when you rest the pointer on it or tap the logo, with Search at the top.
- Mac: bigger text in the guide, the player fills the window (full screen if you choose it in Settings → Player), volume with the scroll wheel, kept between channels, and ⌘1 to ⌘5 to move between sections.

## Version 0.7

4 November 2026

- Spotlight: search a channel, or a programme on your favorites, from the system search. Settings → General to turn it off.
- Siri: “Search the guide in Smartivi”. It asks what you want to watch and plays it if it’s on, or tells you when it starts.
- iPhone: controls for Control Center, the Lock Screen and the Action button, to open the last channel or one you choose.

## Version 0.6

4 November 2026

- Help in Settings: every feature explained, with search. On Apple TV, read it on your phone with a QR code.
- New settings: categories A to Z, hide Favorites or Recent, which logo shows first, update all playlists, and left and right on the remote.
- Fixes: the player could stop answering touches, Mac player controls come back when you move the mouse, and the iPad guide in portrait.

## Version 0.5

4 November 2026

- Logos for channels whose playlist has none or a broken one, from open logo collections.
- Logos are kept on the device, so they still show if the provider’s server goes down.
- Channel numbers go 1, 2, 3… in each category, so you can tell the first from the last. The provider’s numbers are in Settings → Appearance.
- Quick buttons at the top right of the guide: hours on screen, text size, channels on screen and the preview.
- Apple TV: the Smartivi logo at the top of the side bar.

## Version 0.4

4 November 2026

- Countries: in playlists that mix countries, choose which ones you see (playlist menu → Countries…).
- Apple TV: the first column shows your playlist, Favorites, Recent and every category with its number of channels.
- What’s new: this page, also in Settings.
- Fixes for the sleep timer, reminders and channel logos.

## Version 0.3

3 November 2026

- Tonight’s best: the top-rated programmes on your channels tonight.
- Reminders ahead of time, or switching to the channel by themselves.
- Tidy names in big playlists: “ES - FUTBOL” becomes “🇪🇸 Fútbol”.
- On Demand for huge catalogs: categories, My list, history and sorting.
- Subtitles for movies and series from OpenSubtitles.
- Sleep timer, picture fit, a clock in the player and your preferred audio and subtitle language.
- Programme details on a card, and Settings in sections.

## Version 0.2

2 November 2026

- Movies and series from your playlists.
- Multiview: two or four channels at once.
- More colorful themes, text size and settings for each device.

## Version 0.1

1 November 2026

- First beta: guide on every device, catch-up, pausing live TV, iCloud sync, widgets and Siri.

## Before the beta

### 31 October 2026

- Instant start from what’s saved on the device, with Favorites and Recent across your playlists.
- Apple TV: a quick bar in the player, and adding playlists from your phone with a QR code.
- Playback that holds up: audio and subtitle tracks, reconnecting by itself, Picture in Picture and AirPlay.
- The guide on every device, programme search and keyboard shortcuts.
- Settings, channel management and parental controls.
- Catch-up, pausing live TV and reminders.
- iCloud sync and backups.
- The name Smartivi, its icon and this website.
